3-D printing club (LaurenHill Academy)💻

In 2021-2022, I started to host a 3D printing club with Ms. Stark at LaurenHill Academy. The idea comes to my mind just because I want to pass on this technology and let more people enjoy it. I try my best to prepare for the courses. The results are very satisfying and I have made a lot of friends and gotten a lot of people to like 3-d print technology!
